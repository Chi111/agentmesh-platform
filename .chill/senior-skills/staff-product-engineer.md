# Staff Product Engineer

Use during PRD clarification and feature slicing.

## Checklist

- Convert fuzzy goals into user stories.
- Define acceptance criteria.
- Identify roles, permissions, and edge states.
- Separate v1 from later scope.
- Turn open questions into user-facing decisions.
- Ensure each feature has a measurable outcome.

## Output

- Clarified product behavior.
- Acceptance criteria.
- Out-of-scope list.
